# New APHL AMD Training Repository
---
APHL’s Next Generation Sequencing Subcommittee has developed [centralized AMD training repositories](https://airtable.com/app65N2kkVrPrK0WL/shrxVCutYeCiC8B4y) that cover pathogen genomics, wet-laboratory methods, bioinformatics and genomic epidemiology. One repository brings together training from multiple organizations, while [a second view](https://airtable.com/app65N2kkVrPrK0WL/shrrmewvpx7XcFplK) offers suggested training tracks for microbiology, bioinformatics, genomic epidemiology, and pathogen genomics review. Visit [APHL.org/AMD](https://APHL.org/AMD) for more information.
