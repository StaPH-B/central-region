## It’s that time of year again — APHL’s Centralized AMD Needs Assessment is now open!

This year’s survey has been updated to better reflect the needs of our region. Please take a moment to complete it based on your role and responsibilities. **Ideally**, we’d like feedback from *multiple* members of each team.
The information you provide helps us understand how we can best support you and your state. It also allows us to tailor trainings to your needs or identify trainings you may be looking for.

Additionally, please keep an eye out for an email with available dates for our Annual State Calls. Feel free to take notes while you’re filling out the survey for topics to discuss during our calls 

## * * Please complete your survey by October 1, 2026.* * 

Visit <https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/9CPVVZZ> to take this year’s survey!

Let us know if you have any questions!! 

Thanks!
