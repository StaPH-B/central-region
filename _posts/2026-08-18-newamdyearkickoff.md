# 2026-27 Kick-Off and Training Opportunities

### Can you believe it's already time to Kick-Off another year of AMD WFD for the Central Region?

This year, we want to give you more opportunities to collaborate with one another, while also providing new ways for you to ask questions and share information with us. We know that each lab and state has different training and assistance needs. We hope this site provides another platform to help you find the tools and resources you need, when you need them.
Keep an eye out here for:
 - Support Tickets
 - Upcoming Training Opportunity Submission
 - Help Section - share your best practices, hints, and tips that work for you, helpful research studies/papers.
 - And MORE!
